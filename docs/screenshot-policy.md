# IBM Bob Screenshot Policy

Screenshots are evidence-bearing documentation, not decoration.

A screenshot should demonstrate at least one of: a real product capability, an Agentic Strangler stage, evidence produced, or a human decision/gate remaining after the tool action.

Requirements: synthetic or authorized content only; capture date; no proprietary/customer data; product/version context where practical; and required brand/permission review before publication.

No IBM Bob screenshots are included in v0.1.0.
